VISION

The Future

To be the voice for children living in red-light areas — providing education, opportunities, and a future free from the cycle of forced prostitution.

At JAAGO, we stand for the rights, dignity, and dreams of the most vulnerable.

The vision is to break the cycle of second-generation prostitution by creating pathways to quality education, better health, safe environments, and empowering opportunities.

Through awareness, community support, and relentless action, we aim to build a future where every child can thrive, dream, and reclaim their right to choose their life.
